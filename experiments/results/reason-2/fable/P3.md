**Schedule**

- 9am: Dev
- 10am: Aisha
- 11am: Carla
- 12pm: Ben
- 1pm: Elena

**Why this is the only solution**

The key is constraint 2 (Carla–Ben as a consecutive pair) combined with constraint 5 (Elena after Ben). Checking each possible placement of the Carla/Ben pair:

- **Carla 9, Ben 10:** Elena must be 12pm or 1pm (not 11am by rule 4). Dev must precede Aisha in the two remaining slots, which forces Aisha into 12pm or 1pm, directly adjacent to Elena. Violates rule 6.
- **Carla 10, Ben 11:** Elena is 12pm or 1pm; the remaining slots are 9am and the other of 12pm/1pm. Aisha can't take 9am (rule 1), so Dev is 9am and Aisha is adjacent to Elena. Violates rule 6.
- **Carla 12pm, Ben 1pm:** No slot after Ben for Elena. Violates rule 5.
- **Carla 11, Ben 12pm:** Elena must be 1pm. Remaining slots 9am and 10am go to Dev and Aisha; Dev must be earlier (rule 3), so Dev 9am, Aisha 10am. Aisha (10am) and Elena (1pm) are not adjacent, and Elena is not at 11am. All six constraints hold.

Only the last case survives, so the schedule above is the unique answer.
