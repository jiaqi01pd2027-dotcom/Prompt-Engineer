# Solution

**9am – Dev**
**10am – Aisha**
**11am – Carla**
**12pm – Ben**
**1pm – Elena**

**Why this works (and is the only fit):**

- Constraint 2 (Ben right after Carla) only allows the pairs (9,10), (10,11), or (11,12) for (Carla, Ben) — since constraint 5 (Elena later than Ben) rules out Ben in the last slot.
- Testing (Carla=9, Ben=10) and (Carla=10, Ben=11): in every valid arrangement of the remaining people, Aisha and Elena end up adjacent, violating constraint 6. Both cases fail.
- That leaves (Carla=11, Ben=12). Constraint 5 then forces Elena=1pm (the only slot later than 12pm). Constraint 4 (Elena ≠ 11am) is automatically satisfied.
- The remaining slots, 9am and 10am, go to Dev and Aisha. Constraint 1 (Aisha ≠ 9am) forces Aisha=10am, Dev=9am — which also satisfies constraint 3 (Dev earlier than Aisha).
- Final check on constraint 6: Aisha (10am) and Elena (1pm) are two slots apart — not adjacent. All six constraints check out, and this is the unique solution.
