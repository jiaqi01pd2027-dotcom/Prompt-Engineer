## Solution

**9am – Dev, 10am – Aisha, 11am – Carla, 12pm – Ben, 1pm – Elena**

### Reasoning (most restrictive clues first)

Clues 2 and 5 together force a strict order **Carla < Ben < Elena** (Ben immediately after Carla, and Elena strictly after Ben). Let Carla's position be *c*; then Ben = *c*+1, and Elena must be > *c*+1 and ≠ 11am (pos 3, clue 4). This leaves only these skeletons:

| Carla | Ben | Elena | possible |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (9am) | 2 (10am) | 4 or 5 |
| 2 (10am) | 3 (11am) | 4 or 5 |
| 3 (11am) | 4 (12pm) | 5 only |
| 4 (12pm) | 5 (1pm) | impossible (no slot left for Elena) |

For each surviving skeleton, the two leftover slots go to Aisha and Dev, constrained by clue 3 (Dev before Aisha), clue 1 (Aisha ≠ 9am), and clue 6 (Aisha/Elena not adjacent):

- **Carla=9, Ben=10, Elena=4:** leftover slots {11,1pm} → only valid Dev/Aisha order is Dev=11, Aisha=1pm, but then |Aisha−Elena| = |5−4| = 1 → violates clue 6. Fails.
- **Carla=9, Ben=10, Elena=1pm(5):** leftover {11,12} → Dev=11, Aisha=12 works for clues 1/3, but |Aisha−Elena|=|4−5|=1 → violates clue 6. Fails.
- **Carla=10, Ben=11, Elena=12:** leftover {9,1pm} → Aisha≠9am forces Aisha=1pm(5), Dev=9am(1); |Aisha−Elena|=|5−4|=1 → violates clue 6. Fails.
- **Carla=10, Ben=11, Elena=1pm(5):** leftover {9,12} → Aisha≠9am forces Aisha=12(4), Dev=9am(1); |Aisha−Elena|=|4−5|=1 → violates clue 6. Fails.
- **Carla=11, Ben=12, Elena=1pm(5):** leftover {9,10} → Aisha≠9am forces Aisha=10(2), Dev=9am(1); |Aisha−Elena|=|2−5|=3 → satisfies clue 6. **Works.**

Every other branch of the search tree is eliminated, so this is the unique solution.
