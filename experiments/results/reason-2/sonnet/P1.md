## Solution

| Slot | Presenter |
|------|-----------|
| 9am | **Dev** |
| 10am | **Aisha** |
| 11am | **Carla** |
| 12pm | **Ben** |
| 1pm | **Elena** |

**Verification against all constraints:**

1. Aisha is at 10am, not 9am. ✓
2. Ben (12pm) is immediately after Carla (11am). ✓
3. Dev (9am) is earlier than Aisha (10am). ✓
4. Elena is at 1pm, not 11am. ✓
5. Elena (1pm) is later than Ben (12pm). ✓
6. Aisha (10am) and Elena (1pm) are three slots apart — not adjacent. ✓

This is the unique solution: checking the only possible Carla/Ben adjacent pairs (since Elena must be later than Ben, Ben can't be in the last slot), the pairs (9am/10am) and (10am/11am) for Carla/Ben both force Aisha and Elena into adjacent slots, violating constraint 6. Only Carla=11am/Ben=12pm survives, which then forces Elena=1pm, Dev=9am, Aisha=10am.
