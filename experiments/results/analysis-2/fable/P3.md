## Why TaskForge

- **Both must-haves on one plan.** Gantt and native GitHub are included in Pro; there's no higher tier to be pushed onto.
- **Fits the budget at 8 and 10.** $9 × 8 = $72, $9 × 10 = $90. When the contractors leave you drop back to $72 with no penalty, since there are no minimum seats and no annual lock-in. It even has room for one more seat ($99) before crossing $100.
- **Monthly and annual cost the same**, so finance's monthly-only rule costs you nothing — unlike Planlet, where monthly billing carries a 25% surcharge.
- **Nothing else in the datasheet is a constraint for you**: 100 GB storage against 5 GB used, unlimited automations you barely need, 30-day trial to validate the GitHub link with real PRs.

One thing to note: guests count as full seats, so if you later want stakeholders (a director, a client) to have view-only access, each one is another $9. The team as described doesn't need that today.

## Why not the others

**Planlet — over budget.** Gantt is gated to the Business plan, and Business on monthly billing is $15/user: $120 for 8, $150 for 10, both over $100. The Team plan ($8/user, $64–$80) would fit the budget and has GitHub, but it has no timeline view, which rules it out given sprints are planned on a timeline. The only way to hit $100 on Business is annual billing ($12 × 8 = $96), which finance won't approve — and that still breaks at 10 users ($120). Its free view-only guests wouldn't help, since the contractors will be doing work, not just commenting.

**HiveMind Boards — no GitHub.** It's the cheapest option by a wide margin ($49 flat for up to 10) and has Gantt on every plan, but the datasheet explicitly states there is no native GitHub or GitLab integration. With every task linked to a PR, that's disqualifying. Zapier is listed, but the datasheet doesn't say what it can do with GitHub, and you asked me not to assume unlisted features — plus it would be a second subscription and a maintenance burden for a team that rarely uses automations. Secondary issue: the 11th user forces an upgrade to Growth ($99), so there's no headroom beyond the two contractors.
