## Recommendation

Sign up for TaskForge Pro on monthly billing: it is the only tool that meets both must-haves (Gantt view and native GitHub integration) while staying under $100/month at 8 users ($72) and at 10 users ($90).

## Why not the others

- **Planlet:** The Gantt/timeline view is available on Business only, and Business billed monthly is $15 per user, so 8 users cost $120 and 10 users cost $150, both over the $100 monthly cap. The Team plan fits the budget ($64 at 8, $80 at 10) but has no Gantt view, so it fails a must-have.
- **Hivemind Boards:** Starter is the cheapest option at a flat $49 for up to 10 users and includes Gantt, but the datasheet states there is no native GitHub or GitLab integration (only Slack, Google Drive and Zapier), which fails the requirement that every task link natively to a GitHub pull request. Adding an 11th user would also force the $99 Growth plan.

Notes for sign-up: TaskForge's 5 GB of attachments fits comfortably in its 100 GB workspace storage, the team rarely uses automations so unlimited automations is a bonus rather than a factor, and because guests count as full paid seats, any external reviewer should be budgeted as an additional $9 user. The 30-day trial lets the team confirm the GitHub link and Gantt view before the first monthly charge.
