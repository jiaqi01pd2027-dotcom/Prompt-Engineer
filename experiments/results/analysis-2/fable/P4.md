**Recommendation: TaskForge Pro.**

**Monthly totals (monthly billing)**

| Tool / plan | 8 users | 10 users | Gantt | Native GitHub |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Planlet Team | $64 | $80 | No | Yes |
| Planlet Business | $120 | $150 | Yes | Yes |
| TaskForge Pro | $72 | $90 | Yes | Yes |
| Hivemind Starter | $49 | $49 | Yes | No |

**Why TaskForge**

It is the only tool that meets every hard requirement inside the budget. Pro includes Kanban, list, calendar and Gantt views, native GitHub (plus GitLab, Slack and Figma) integration, unlimited automations, 100 GB storage, built-in time tracking and an API (1,000 requests/hour per workspace). Pricing is $9 per user/month with no annual discount, so monthly billing costs nothing extra, and there is no minimum seat count. At 8 users the team pays $72; growing to 10 raises it to $90, still under $100. The 30-day trial is the longest of the three.

Limits to be aware of: guests count as full paid seats, so each external collaborator adds $9/month. With 10 members, that leaves room for only one guest before exceeding $100.

**Why not Planlet**

Planlet's Gantt/timeline view is restricted to the Business plan, which costs $15 per user/month on monthly billing: $120 at 8 users and $150 at 10, both over budget. The Team plan ($8/user, $64 at 8 and $80 at 10) fits the budget and has GitHub, but lacks Gantt, caps automations at 250 runs/month and storage at 10 GB. Its unlimited free guests and 14-day trial do not compensate for missing a required view. Time tracking is not available at all.

**Why not Hivemind Boards**

Hivemind is the cheapest option: Starter is a flat $49/month for up to 10 users, with Gantt on all plans, 1,000 automation runs/month, 25 GB storage and 5 free guests. But it has no native GitHub or GitLab integration, only Slack, Google Drive and Zapier, which fails a hard requirement. Time tracking is likewise only available through a Zapier connection to a third-party tool. Note also that the 10-user cap is exact: an 11th user forces an upgrade to Growth at $99/month.

**Bottom line**

Choose TaskForge Pro at $72/month now and $90/month at 10 users, and budget $9 per guest seat.
