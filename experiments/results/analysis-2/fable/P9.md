**Comparison (monthly billing, per the datasheets)**

| | Planlet | TaskForge | HiveMind Boards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan needed for Gantt | Business ($15/user/mo monthly) | Pro ($9/user/mo, same monthly or annual) | Starter ($49/mo flat, up to 10 users) |
| Cost at 8 users | $120/mo | $72/mo | $49/mo |
| Cost at 10 users | $150/mo | $90/mo | $49/mo |
| Gantt/timeline | Business only | Yes | Yes (all plans) |
| Native GitHub | Yes (Team and above) | Yes (plus GitLab) | No (Slack, Google Drive, Zapier only) |
| Automations | Unlimited on Business | Unlimited | 1,000 runs/mo on Starter |
| Storage | 100 GB on Business | 100 GB | 25 GB on Starter |
| Guests | Unlimited free (view/comment) | Count as paid seats | Up to 5 free |
| Time tracking | Not available | Built in | Only via Zapier to a third-party tracker |
| Trial | 14-day Business | 30-day | 14-day |
| Other limits | 3-seat minimum on paid plans | API 1,000 requests/hour/workspace | 11th user forces Growth ($99/mo) |

Your storage (about 5 GB) and automation needs (rare) are comfortably within every option, so they don't separate the three.

**Recommendation: TaskForge Pro**

It is the only tool that meets both must-haves *and* your budget on monthly billing:

- Gantt and native GitHub are both included on Pro.
- $72/month at 8 users and $90/month at 10 stays under the $100 hard cap in both scenarios, with no annual commitment and no seat minimum.
- The 30-day trial is the longest of the three, and time tracking is built in if you ever want it.

One thing to note: guests count as full paid seats, so the two contractors will be $9 each. That is already reflected in the $90 figure for 10 users.

**Why the other two are out**

- **Planlet**: Gantt is Business-only, and Business on monthly billing is $15/user, so $120 at 8 users and $150 at 10. Both exceed the $100 cap. The cheaper Team plan lacks Gantt, and the $6/$12 rates require the annual billing finance won't approve.
- **HiveMind Boards**: Cheapest by far ($49 flat covers all 10 users), and it has Gantt, but it has no native GitHub or GitLab integration. That is a stated must-have, and the datasheet offers no native workaround (only Zapier). It also cannot go past 10 users without jumping to the $99 Growth plan, though that still fits your cap.
