## Why TaskForge

- **Pricing:** Pro is $9 per user/month with no minimum seats and no annual discount to tempt you into a commitment. 8 seats = $72/month; 10 seats = $90/month. Both under $100. 30-day trial.
- **Features:** Kanban, list, calendar and Gantt on Pro; native GitHub (and GitLab) integration; unlimited automations; built-in time tracking; 100 GB storage per workspace.
- **Limits to be aware of:**
  - **Guests count as full paid seats.** This is the one real trap. If you rely on outside stakeholders viewing boards, each one is another $9. At 10 team members ($90) you have room for exactly one guest before you cross $100 ($99 at 11 seats).
  - API is capped at 1,000 requests per hour per workspace. Fine for a team of 10 unless you plan heavy scripted syncs.

## Why not the other two

**Planlet** – ruled out on price. Its GitHub integration and monthly billing are fine, but the Gantt/timeline view is on Business only, and Business billed monthly is $15 per user/month: $120 for 8 people and $150 for 10, both over your $100 cap. The Team plan ($8/user monthly, $64–$80) fits the budget and has GitHub, but has no Gantt, so it fails a hard requirement. The $12 Business rate that would fit at 8 users ($96) requires annual billing, which you have excluded, and it would break the budget anyway at 10 users ($120). Note that Planlet does have one thing TaskForge lacks: unlimited free guests (view and comment only).

**HiveMind Boards** – ruled out on integration. Starter at $49/month flat for up to 10 users is the cheapest option and includes Gantt on all plans, but it has no native GitHub or GitLab integration; the only route is Zapier, which is not native and adds another paid tool and another point of failure. Time tracking is likewise only via Zapier. If GitHub integration ever stops being a requirement, HiveMind Starter becomes the value pick, and its 11th-user cliff (forced upgrade to Growth at $99) would still sit within your budget.

## Bottom line

Go with TaskForge Pro at $72/month for 8, scaling to $90/month at 10, on monthly billing. Manage guest access deliberately, since every guest is a paid seat. Use the 30-day trial to confirm the Gantt view and GitHub sync behave the way your team expects before committing.
