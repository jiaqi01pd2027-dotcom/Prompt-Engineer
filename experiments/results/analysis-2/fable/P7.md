**Recommendation: TaskForge Pro.** It is the only tool that meets all four requirements. At $9/user with identical monthly and annual pricing, it costs $72 now and $90 at 10 users, staying under budget with no annual commitment. It has Gantt, a native GitHub integration (plus GitLab), unlimited automations, 100 GB storage, built-in time tracking, and a 30-day trial. Two caveats: guests count as full paid seats, so each external collaborator adds $9/month (one guest at 10 users would hit $99, two would exceed the budget), and the API is capped at 1,000 requests/hour per workspace.

**Why not Planlet:** Gantt/timeline is gated to the Business plan, and monthly Business billing is $15/user. That is $120 for 8 people and $150 for 10, 20-50% over budget. Getting under $100 would require annual billing ($12/user = $96 at 8 users), which fails the no-annual-commitment requirement and would still break the budget at 10 users ($120). Its unlimited free guests and GitHub integration are attractive, but the Gantt gating makes it unaffordable on monthly terms.

**Why not Hivemind Boards:** It is the cheapest option and the flat $49 Starter covers growth to exactly 10 users with Gantt included, but it has no native GitHub or GitLab integration; the only route is through Zapier, which is a third-party workaround rather than a native integration and adds a separate Zapier cost and maintenance burden. It also has the tightest automation limit (1,000 runs/month) and 25 GB of storage, and an 11th user would force the $99 Growth plan.
