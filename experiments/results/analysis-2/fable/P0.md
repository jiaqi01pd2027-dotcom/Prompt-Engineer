There's no single winner — the listing doesn't say what you need, and the three are built for different situations. Here's how they split:

**TaskForge** — best all-round feature set. Every view (including Gantt), unlimited automations, built-in time tracking, GitHub *and* GitLab, 100 GB, no seat minimum, 30-day trial. The catch: guests are paid seats, so if you collaborate with many clients/contractors, costs balloon. $9/user flat.

**Hivemind Boards** — best value for 8–25 people. $49 flat for up to 10 users (~$4.90/user), $99 for up to 25 (~$3.96/user), less with annual billing. Gantt on every plan. The catch: no GitHub/GitLab integration, time tracking only through Zapier, and only 5 free guests. Also a hard cliff: user #11 jumps you from $49 to $99.

**Planlet** — best for very small or guest-heavy teams. Free for up to 5 users / 3 projects, cheapest paid tier ($6–8/user), and unlimited free guests. The catch: Gantt requires Business ($12–15/user), automations are capped at 250/month on Team, 3-seat minimum, and no time tracking at all.

**Quick decision guide:**
- ≤5 people, ≤3 projects → **Planlet Free**
- Software team needing GitHub/GitLab + time tracking → **TaskForge**
- 8–25 people, Gantt needed, no code-repo integration needed → **Hivemind** (cheapest by a wide margin)
- Lots of external guests (clients, reviewers) → **Planlet** (TaskForge charges for each)
- Gantt for a 5–10 person team → Hivemind ($49) beats Planlet Business ($60–150) and TaskForge ($45–90)
