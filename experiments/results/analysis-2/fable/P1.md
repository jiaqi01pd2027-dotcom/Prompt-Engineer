**Recommendation: TaskForge Pro** — it is the only one of the three that meets every hard requirement, and it fits the budget at both 8 and 10 users.

## Why TaskForge

- **Meets all requirements:** Gantt is included on Pro, GitHub integration is native, and the $9 price is identical on monthly and annual billing, so you lose nothing by staying month-to-month.
- **Headroom:** $90 at 10 users leaves ~$10 of budget. No minimum seats and per-user pricing means you pay only for the people you actually add.
- **Extras you get for free:** unlimited automations, 100 GB storage, built-in time tracking, GitLab and Figma integrations, and an API (1,000 requests/hour). 30-day trial is the longest of the three.
- **Limits to watch:** guests count as full paid seats. At 10 users you can afford exactly one guest ($99) before crossing $100. If you routinely need external stakeholders to view or comment, that is the one place TaskForge is weaker than the others.

## Why not Planlet

Gantt/timeline is Business-only. Business on monthly billing is $15/user, which is **$120 at 8 users and $150 at 10** — over budget either way. Even the annual rate ($12/user) would be $96 at 8 users and $120 at 10, and that requires the annual commitment you want to avoid. The Team plan fits the budget ($64–$80) and has GitHub, but it has no Gantt view, so it fails the core requirement. Other notes: 3-seat minimum (irrelevant at your size), no time tracking, 250 automation runs/month on Team, but unlimited free view/comment guests, which is its one clear advantage.

## Why not Hivemind Boards

It is the cheapest option ($49 flat for up to 10 users, Gantt on all plans), but it has **no native GitHub or GitLab integration**. The only path is Zapier, which is a third-party workaround, adds another subscription to manage, and would eat into the 1,000 automation runs/month on Starter. That fails a stated hard requirement. Also note that the 11th user forces an upgrade to Growth at $99/month, so it becomes a cliff-edge pricing model if you grow past 10. Time tracking is likewise only available through Zapier. Storage is 25 GB on Starter (vs. 100 GB on TaskForge), and it allows 5 free guests.

## Bottom line

TaskForge Pro at $72/month now and $90/month at 10 users. Use the 30-day trial to confirm the GitHub integration and Gantt view work the way your team expects, and decide up front how you will handle external guests given that they are billed as full seats.
