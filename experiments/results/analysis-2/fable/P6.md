**Requirement:** 8 users growing to 10, Gantt/timeline view, native GitHub integration, monthly billing with no annual commitment, budget $100/month.

| Tool | Plan needed | Cost at 8 users | Cost at 10 users | Gantt | Native GitHub | Monthly billing | Within $100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Planlet | Business (only plan with Gantt) | $120 | $150 | yes | yes | yes ($15/user monthly) | no |
| TaskForge | Pro | $72 | $90 | yes | yes | yes ($9/user, same as annual) | yes |
| Hivemind Boards | Starter (flat, up to 10) | $49 | $49 | yes | no | yes | yes |

**Recommendation: TaskForge Pro.** It is the only tool that meets all four requirements: Gantt is included on Pro, GitHub (and GitLab) integration is native, the $9/user price is identical on monthly billing so there is no penalty for avoiding an annual commitment, and at 10 users it is $90, leaving $10 of headroom. It also has no minimum-seat rule, unlimited automations, 100 GB storage, built-in time tracking, and a 30-day trial.

**Why not the others:**

- **Planlet** is ruled out on budget. Gantt is gated to the Business plan, which on monthly billing is $15/user, so 8 users cost $120 and 10 cost $150, 20% to 50% over the ceiling. The cheaper Team plan ($8/user monthly, $64 for 8) would fit the budget and has the GitHub integration, but it has no Gantt view, so it fails a hard requirement. Annual Business pricing ($12/user, $96 at 8 users) would just squeak in at 8 but still fails at 10 and requires the annual commitment the team does not want.
- **Hivemind Boards** is ruled out on integration. It is the cheapest option ($49 flat covers the whole 8-to-10 range) and has Gantt on all plans, but it has no native GitHub or GitLab integration; the only route is through Zapier, which is not native. Two secondary limits also matter: automations are capped at 1,000 runs/month on Starter, and an 11th user forces a jump to the $99 Growth plan, so growth past 10 would consume the entire budget.

**Limits to watch on TaskForge:** guests count as full paid seats, so any external stakeholders who need access add $9/month each; with 10 members that leaves room for only one guest before exceeding $100. The API is rate-limited to 1,000 requests/hour per workspace. Neither is a blocker for the stated requirements.
