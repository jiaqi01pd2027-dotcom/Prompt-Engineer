## Recommendation

Choose TaskForge Pro on monthly billing: it is the only option that delivers Gantt and native GitHub within the $100 budget at both 8 users ($72) and 10 users ($90), with no annual commitment and no seat minimum.

## Why not the others

- **Planlet**: Gantt is locked to the Business tier, and on monthly billing that is $15 per user, so 8 users already cost $120 and 10 users $150, both over the $100 cap; the cheaper $12 annual rate would need an annual commitment the team does not want (and still hits $120 at 10 users).
- **Hivemind Boards**: The cheapest option at $49 flat and it includes Gantt, but it has no native GitHub integration (only Zapier), which is a hard requirement; it also caps Starter at exactly 10 users, so a single hire beyond the planned growth forces a jump to $99.
