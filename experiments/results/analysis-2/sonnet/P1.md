## Why the other two are ruled out

**Planlet — over budget once Gantt is required.** The Gantt/timeline view exists only on the Business tier, and Business on monthly billing (required, since you don't want an annual commitment) is $15/user/month. For 8 people that's $120/month — already above the $100 ceiling before you even grow to 10 (which would push it to $150/month). The cheaper Team plan ($8/user/month monthly) would fit the budget, but it doesn't have Gantt at all, so it doesn't meet the must-have requirement.

**Hivemind Boards — no native GitHub integration.** Pricing is actually the most attractive of the three (Starter: $49/month flat, covers up to 10 users, includes Gantt on every plan), so it comfortably fits the budget for growth to 10. But GitHub connectivity only exists indirectly through Zapier, not as a native integration — and that's a stated hard requirement. Even setting aside price, this alone disqualifies it (and note the flat-tier structure means an 11th user would force an upgrade to Growth at $99/month, which is a separate scaling concern).

## Recommendation: Taskforge (Pro plan)

Taskforge is the only one of the three that satisfies every stated constraint simultaneously:

- **Gantt/timeline view**: included on Pro.
- **Native GitHub integration**: included (also GitLab and Figma, if useful later).
- **Monthly billing with no annual commitment**: Pro is priced identically whether billed monthly or annually ($9/user/month either way), so there's no financial penalty for staying month-to-month — unlike Planlet, where avoiding an annual contract costs significantly more per seat.
- **Budget**: $72/month at 8 users, rising to only $90/month at 10 users — comfortably under the $100/month cap, with no seat minimums to worry about.

One limit worth flagging: Taskforge counts guests as full paid seats (unlike Planlet's free guests or Hivemind's 5 free guests), so if the team plans to bring in external collaborators or clients as guests, factor that into the per-seat cost before committing.
