## Comparison

**PLANLET**
- Pricing (monthly billing, as required): Team $8/user/mo, Business $15/user/mo, 3-seat minimum.
- Gantt/timeline: **Business plan only**.
- Native GitHub: Team and above.
- To get Gantt, you need Business → 8 users = $120/mo, 10 users = $150/mo.
- Storage 100GB (Business), unlimited automations (Business) — well beyond your ~5GB/rare-automation needs.

**TASKFORGE**
- Pricing: Pro $9/user/mo, identical whether billed monthly or annually, no seat minimum.
- Gantt/timeline: included on Pro.
- Native GitHub: included on Pro.
- 8 users = $72/mo; 10 users = $90/mo.
- Storage 100GB, unlimited automations, built-in time tracking, guests count as full paid seats (not a factor for you since you don't need guest access called out).

**HIVEMIND BOARDS**
- Pricing: Starter flat $49/mo for up to 10 users (covers you at both 8 and 10 without a plan change); Growth $99/mo for up to 25.
- Gantt/timeline: included on all plans.
- Native GitHub: **not available** — only Slack, Google Drive, and Zapier; GitHub would require a Zapier workaround, not a native integration.
- Storage 25GB on Starter — enough for your ~5GB.

## Recommendation: TASKFORGE

Both of your must-haves — Gantt/timeline view and native GitHub integration — are must-haves, and Taskforge is the only tool that clears both without a plan upgrade:

- **Planlet is out**: its Gantt view is locked to the Business plan, which costs $15/user/month on monthly billing (annual is off the table per Finance). That's $120/month at 8 users and $150/month at 10 — both blow past your $100 hard cap.
- **Hivemind Boards is out**: it's the cheapest option in absolute terms ($49/month flat, comfortably under budget at both 8 and 10 users) and has Gantt built in everywhere, but it has no native GitHub integration — only a Zapier bridge — which fails your explicit must-have.
- **Taskforge fits everything**: Gantt and native GitHub both included on Pro, monthly billing at no penalty (same price as annual), and it's the only option that stays under $100/month at both team sizes — $72/month at 8 users, $90/month at 10 if the contractors join. It also comes in well under your storage needs (100GB vs. ~5GB used) with room to spare.
