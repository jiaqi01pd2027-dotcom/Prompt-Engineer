## Recommendation: TaskForge

### The hard requirements, checked against each tool

**Gantt/timeline view**
- Planlet: "Gantt/timeline view on Business only" — not on Team or Free.
- TaskForge: "Kanban, list, calendar and Gantt on Pro" — included at the only paid tier.
- Hivemind Boards: "Kanban, calendar and Gantt on all plans" — included everywhere.

**Native GitHub integration**
- Planlet: "Integrations: Slack, GitHub, Google Drive" — GitHub is native.
- TaskForge: "Integrations: Slack, GitHub, GitLab, Figma" — GitHub is native.
- Hivemind Boards: "No native GitHub or GitLab integration." This is a hard fail against a stated requirement — Zapier isn't a substitute for "native."

**Monthly billing, no annual commitment, ≤ $100/month for 8 people (and ideally still fits at 10)**
- Planlet: Gantt requires **Business**, and Business "Business: $12 per user/month billed annually, or $15 per user/month billed monthly." Since the team wants monthly billing with no annual lock-in, the applicable rate is $15/user/month. For 8 users that's $120/month — already over budget before even considering growth to 10 ($150/month). The cheaper $12/user annual rate isn't usable because it requires the annual commitment the team wants to avoid.
- TaskForge: "Pro: $9 per user/month, same price on monthly or annual billing." No penalty for going monthly, and "No minimum seats." 8 users = $72/month; growing to 10 users = $90/month. Both comfortably under the $100 cap, with no billing-cycle tradeoff.
- Hivemind Boards: "Starter: $49 per month flat for up to 10 users," with monthly billing available at that flat rate (annual just adds two free months, it doesn't gate the monthly price). This is well within budget and even covers growth to 10 users without an upgrade — but it's disqualified on the GitHub requirement above.

### Why the other two are ruled out

- **Planlet is ruled out on price at the required billing cycle.** The only plan with a Gantt view is Business, and Business billed monthly (the team's requirement) is $15/user/month. For 8 people that's $120/month — 20% over the $100 ceiling — and it gets worse, not better, as the team grows toward 10 ($150/month). The lower $12/user figure only exists under annual billing, which the team explicitly doesn't want.

- **Hivemind Boards is ruled out on the integration requirement, despite winning on price.** At $49/month flat for up to 10 users it's by far the cheapest option and even absorbs the team's growth to 10 without a plan change. But the vendor is explicit: "No native GitHub or GitLab integration." The only path to GitHub data is via Zapier to a third party, which is not a native integration and adds an extra paid dependency and setup/maintenance burden. Since native GitHub integration was a stated requirement, this disqualifies Hivemind regardless of its price advantage.

### Why TaskForge fits

TaskForge is the only tool that clears all four requirements simultaneously: Gantt view included, native GitHub integration included, identical pricing on monthly vs. annual billing (so choosing monthly costs nothing extra), and a total cost that stays under $100/month both today (8 users × $9 = $72/month) and after the team grows to 10 (10 users × $9 = $90/month, still $10 of headroom). It also has no seat minimum, so headcount fluctuations don't force paying for unused seats, and it throws in built-in time tracking and unlimited automations as extras the other two either lack or ration.

One tradeoff worth flagging: TaskForge's guests "count as full paid seats" rather than being free, unlike Planlet's unlimited free guests or Hivemind's 5 free guests. If the team expects to loop in external stakeholders (clients, contractors) as view-only participants, that would add to the monthly cost — worth confirming before committing, since it's the one area where TaskForge is weaker than its competitors.
