## Recommendation

Taskforge on the Pro plan ($9/user/month, no annual commitment) — it costs $72/month at 8 users and $90/month at 10, stays under the $100 cap at both sizes, and includes both Gantt and native GitHub.

## Why not the others

- Planlet: Gantt/timeline view exists only on the Business plan, which costs $120/month at 8 users and $150/month at 10 users even on monthly billing — well over the $100 budget.
- Hivemind Boards: fits the budget easily ($49/month flat) and has Gantt, but has no native GitHub integration (GitHub is reachable only indirectly through Zapier), failing a required feature.
