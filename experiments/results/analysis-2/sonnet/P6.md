## Requirements recap
- 8 people now, possible growth to 10
- Gantt/timeline view required
- Native GitHub integration required (not via a third-party automation connector)
- Monthly billing, no annual commitment
- Budget: ≤ $100/month total

## Comparison

| Tool | Plan needed for Gantt | Billing | Cost at 8 users | Cost at 10 users | Native GitHub | Gantt view |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Planlet | Business | Monthly ($15/user) | $120 | $150 | Yes (Team & above) | Yes, Business only |
| Taskforge | Pro | Monthly ($9/user, same as annual) | $72 | $90 | Yes | Yes |
| Hivemind Boards | Starter (flat, up to 10) | Monthly ($49 flat) | $49 | $49 | No (Zapier only) | Yes, all plans |

## Recommendation: Taskforge Pro

At $9/user/month with no minimum seat count, Taskforge costs **$72/month at 8 people and $90/month at 10** — comfortably under the $100 cap even after the team grows, with room to spare. It has Gantt view on the Pro tier, a native GitHub integration, and its monthly price is identical to the annual price, so there's no "monthly surcharge" or annual lock-in to worry about. Bonus: unlimited automations and built-in time tracking, neither of which the other two offer natively.

## Why the other two are ruled out

- **Planlet — fails the budget requirement.** Gantt/timeline is gated to the Business plan, and Business only comes at $15/user/month on monthly billing (Team at $8/user has GitHub but no Gantt). That's $120/month for 8 people and $150/month for 10 — 20–50% over budget. Switching to annual billing would lower the per-seat rate but violates the "no annual commitment" requirement, so there's no combination of plan/billing that satisfies both constraints.

- **Hivemind Boards — fails the integration requirement.** Its Starter plan is cheap ($49 flat, covers up to 10 users) and includes Gantt on every tier, but it has no native GitHub integration at all — only a Zapier bridge to third-party tools, which is not a native connection and adds setup fragility and potential extra cost/limits from Zapier itself. Price isn't the issue here; the missing core integration is a hard disqualifier. (Worth noting separately: if the team ever exceeds 10 users, Starter forces an upgrade to the $99/month Growth plan — which would still lack native GitHub.)
