## Step-by-step check

**Planlet** — Gantt is Business-only (Team lacks it), so the cheapest qualifying plan is Business, monthly billing = $15/user/month.
- 8 users: 8 × $15 = **$120/month**
- 10 users: 10 × $15 = **$150/month**
Both exceed the $100 budget → **fails budget check**, ruled out even though it has both required features.

**Taskforge** — Only Pro has Gantt; Pro also has native GitHub. Price is $9/user/month, identical on monthly or annual billing, no seat minimum.
- 8 users: 8 × $9 = **$72/month**
- 10 users: 10 × $9 = **$90/month**
Both under $100 → **passes budget check**.

**Hivemind Boards** — All plans have Gantt, but there is **no native GitHub integration** on any plan (only Slack, Google Drive, Zapier). Since native GitHub is a hard requirement, Hivemind is disqualified on features before pricing even matters. (For reference, Starter is $49/month flat for up to 10 users and would have been well within budget — it's the integration gap that rules it out, not price.)

Only one candidate survives both the feature filter and the budget filter, so there's no head-to-head limits comparison needed among multiple qualifying tools — but the limits are noted below for completeness.

## Recommendation: TaskForge (Pro plan)

**Why it wins:**
- Has both required capabilities: Gantt/timeline view and native GitHub integration, on the Pro plan.
- Monthly billing costs the same as annual ($9/user/month) — no penalty for avoiding an annual commitment.
- Cost fits the budget with margin: **$72/month at 8 users, $90/month at 10 users**, both under the $100 cap, and no minimum-seat surcharge to worry about.

**Other Taskforge details worth knowing:**
- Unlimited automations, 100 GB workspace storage, built-in time tracking (a bonus feature not required but useful).
- API capped at 1,000 requests/hour per workspace — fine for normal team use, worth watching only if you build heavy integrations later.
- Guests count as full paid seats — if you ever add outside collaborators, each one adds $9/month rather than being free, unlike Planlet's free guest access.

**Why the other two are ruled out:**
- **Planlet**: The only plan with Gantt (Business) costs $120/month at 8 users and $150/month at 10 — over budget by a wide margin on monthly billing. Its cheaper Team plan ($8/user/month, $64–80/month) would fit the budget but doesn't include Gantt at all.
- **Hivemind Boards**: Priced attractively ($49/month flat covers up to 10 users, well under budget) and has Gantt on every plan, but it has no native GitHub integration on any plan — only a Zapier-mediated workaround for other functions, and no GitHub/GitLab connector at all. That disqualifies it on a hard requirement regardless of price.
