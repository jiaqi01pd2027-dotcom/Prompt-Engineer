The "best" one depends on what you're optimizing for — there's no single winner across price, features, and team size. Here's the breakdown:

## Quick take by priority

**Need native GitHub/GitLab integration + time tracking + Gantt on one paid tier, small team:**
→ **TaskForge**. Its single $9/user/mo Pro plan bundles Gantt, GitHub, GitLab, Figma, unlimited automations, and built-in time tracking with no seat minimum. It's the only one with real time tracking (the others rely on Zapier or lack it) and the only one with GitLab/Figma.
→ Downside: guests count as full paid seats, so it gets expensive fast if you loop in clients/stakeholders.

**Small team (3–5 people), tight budget, external guests/clients need visibility:**
→ **Planlet**. Free tier covers up to 5 users/3 projects. If you outgrow that, Team is the cheapest paid seat price ($6/user/mo annual) and guests are unlimited and free (view/comment). Tradeoff: no time tracking at all, and Gantt/timeline is Business-only.

**Larger team (10–25 people), cost-sensitive, don't need GitHub:**
→ **Hivemind Boards**. Flat pricing crushes per-seat pricing at scale — Growth is $99/mo flat for up to 25 users (~$4/user), versus ~$150–225/mo for the same headcount on Planlet or TaskForge. Gantt is included on every plan, even Starter. Tradeoff: no native GitHub or GitLab integration (only Zapier), and time tracking is also Zapier-only (extra tool + cost).

Hivemind wins on raw cost once you're past ~7 users; Planlet wins for very small teams; TaskForge is priciest per-seat but has the deepest dev-tooling integrations.
