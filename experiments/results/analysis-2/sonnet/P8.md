# Tool Recommendation

| Tool | Plan needed | Monthly cost at 8 users | Monthly cost at 10 users | Gantt | Native GitHub | Key limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Planlet | Business (monthly billing) | $15/user × 8 = **$120** | $15/user × 10 = **$150** | Yes (Business only) | Yes | Business is the only plan with Gantt, but its monthly price puts the team over the $100 cap at both 8 and 10 users; minimum 3 seats; 100 GB storage. |
| Taskforge | Pro | $9/user × 8 = **$72** | $9/user × 10 = **$90** | Yes (Pro) | Yes | Guests count as full paid seats; API capped at 1,000 requests/hour per workspace; 100 GB storage. |
| Hivemind Boards | Starter (flat, up to 10 users) | **$49** flat | **$49** flat | Yes (all plans) | No — only via Zapier | No native GitHub or GitLab integration; time tracking only through a Zapier connection to a third-party tracker; 25 GB storage; 11th user forces upgrade to Growth. |

## Recommendation

Choose Taskforge on the Pro plan: it is the only option that includes both a native GitHub integration and a Gantt/timeline view while staying under the $100/month budget at both 8 users ($72) and the peak of 10 users ($90).

## Why not the others

- **Planlet**: Gantt/timeline is a must-have, but it only exists on the Business plan, which costs $120/month at 8 users and $150/month at 10 users billed monthly — both exceed the $100 cap; the Team plan fits the budget but has no Gantt view.
- **Hivemind Boards**: Comfortably fits the budget at a flat $49/month for up to 10 users and includes Gantt on every plan, but it has no native GitHub integration — GitHub can only be reached indirectly through Zapier, which fails the team's must-have requirement and doesn't fit their stated low use of automations.
