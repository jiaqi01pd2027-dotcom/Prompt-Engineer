## Recommendation: TaskForge Pro

**TaskForge Pro** is the right fit: $9 per user/month, same price whether billed monthly or annually, no seat minimum.
- 8 users: 8 × $9 = **$72/month**
- 10 users: 10 × $9 = **$90/month**

Both totals are under the $100 cap, comfortably covering growth to 10 without a plan change. Pro includes Gantt view (alongside Kanban, list, calendar) and a native GitHub integration, plus GitLab and Figma. Automations are unlimited, storage is 100 GB, time tracking is built in, and there's an API (1,000 req/hour/workspace). The only caveat is that guests count as full paid seats, but that doesn't affect this team's stated headcount.

### Why Planlet is ruled out
Gantt/timeline view only exists on the **Business** plan, and Business billed monthly costs $15 per user/month (the Team plan is cheaper but has no Gantt view):
- 8 users: 8 × $15 = **$120/month**
- 10 users: 10 × $15 = **$150/month**

Both exceed the $100/month budget, so Planlet fails on price even though it does have native GitHub integration and a monthly-billing option.

### Why Hivemind Boards is ruled out
Pricing is actually attractive: Starter is $49/month flat for up to 10 users, so both team sizes stay on Starter:
- 8 users: **$49/month**
- 10 users: **$49/month** (10 is within the "up to 10" cap)

That's well within budget, and Gantt is included on all plans. However, Hivemind Boards has **no native GitHub integration** — GitHub isn't listed among its integrations (Slack, Google Drive, Zapier only), and connecting to GitHub would require a third-party Zapier workaround rather than a native integration. Since native GitHub integration is a stated requirement, Hivemind Boards is disqualified regardless of its price advantage.

### Summary
Only TaskForge Pro satisfies all four requirements simultaneously: Gantt view, native GitHub integration, monthly billing with no annual commitment, and a total cost under $100/month at both 8 and 10 users ($72 and $90 respectively).
