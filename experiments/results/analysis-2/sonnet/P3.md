## Comparison

**Must-haves for this team:** Gantt/timeline view, native GitHub integration, monthly billing (no annual lock-in), ≤$100/month for up to 10 users.

### Planlet — ruled out
- Gantt/timeline view exists **only on the Business plan** (Team plan lacks it).
- Business, billed monthly (no annual commitment): **$15/user/month**.
  - 8 users: $120/month
  - 10 users: $150/month
- Both exceed the $100/month cap. The cheaper Team tier ($8/user/month monthly, $64–$80/month) fits the budget but has no Gantt view — and Gantt is a must-have, not optional.
- Even the annual Business rate ($12/user/month → $96/month at 8 users) only fits by taking on the annual contract the team already got burned by and finance has ruled out.
- **Verdict:** Fails on budget-with-monthly-billing whenever Gantt is included. Ruled out.

### Hivemind Boards — ruled out
- Pricing is attractive and flat: Starter is $49/month flat for up to 10 users (covers the contractor bump with no per-seat math), Growth $99/month for up to 25. Both fit the $100 budget easily, and monthly billing is native (no annual push needed).
- Has Gantt view on all plans.
- **But: no native GitHub integration.** Listed integrations are Slack, Google Drive, and Zapier only — GitHub/GitLab aren't offered. Since every task needs to link to a GitHub PR, and the datasheet doesn't list any GitHub connector (native or otherwise), this fails a must-have. Bridging through Zapier isn't a native integration and isn't documented as covering GitHub, so it can't be assumed.
- **Verdict:** Best price, but disqualified by the missing GitHub integration.

### Taskforge — recommended
- Gantt/timeline view included on Pro, alongside Kanban/list/calendar.
- **Native GitHub integration** included on Pro (also GitLab and Figma, unused here but no harm).
- Pricing: **$9/user/month, identical whether billed monthly or annually** — so monthly billing costs nothing extra and there's no annual-commitment trap.
  - 8 users: $72/month
  - 10 users (with both contractors on): $90/month
  - Both comfortably under the $100 cap, with $10–28/month of headroom.
- No minimum seat count, so the two contractors can be added and dropped for just the one quarter without penalty.
- Storage: 100 GB per workspace vs. the team's ~5 GB of attachments — no constraint.
- Automations are unlimited, but the team barely uses them, so this is a non-factor either way (not a differentiator, just confirms no downside).
- One caveat worth flagging to the team: guests count as full paid seats on Taskforge (unlike Planlet's free guests or Hivemind's 5 free guests). If they ever want outside stakeholders to view/comment without paying, that's a real limit — but nothing in the brief indicates they need guest access, so it doesn't affect this recommendation.

## Recommendation

**Taskforge Pro.** It's the only one of the three that satisfies all four hard requirements simultaneously: Gantt view, native GitHub integration, true monthly billing at no price penalty, and a total cost ($72/month at 8 users, rising to $90/month with both contractors) that stays under $100/month with room to spare. Planlet is ruled out because Gantt is locked behind a Business tier that busts the budget under monthly billing. Hivemind Boards is ruled out because, despite the best price, it has no native GitHub integration, which the team treats as non-negotiable since every task links to a PR.
