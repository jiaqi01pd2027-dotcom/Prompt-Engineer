**Comparison**

| | Planlet | TaskForge | HiveMind Boards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gantt/timeline | Business plan only | Yes, on Pro | Yes, all plans |
| Native GitHub | Yes (Team+) | Yes (plus GitLab, Figma) | No — Zapier only |
| Monthly price | Team $8/user; Business $15/user (min 3 seats) | $9/user, monthly = annual | Starter $49 flat ≤10 users; Growth $99 flat ≤25 |
| Cost at 8 / 10 users (monthly) | $120 / $150 (Business) | $72 / $90 | $49 / $49 |
| Automations | 250 runs (Team), unlimited (Business) | Unlimited | 1,000 (Starter) |
| Storage | 10 GB (Team) / 100 GB (Business) | 100 GB | 25 GB (Starter) |
| Guests | Unlimited free, view/comment | Count as full paid seats | Up to 5 free |
| Time tracking | None | Built in | Via Zapier only |
| Trial | 14 days (Business) | 30 days | 14 days |

**Recommendation: TaskForge Pro.**

It is the only option that meets both must-haves and the billing constraint at once. Gantt is included on Pro, GitHub is a native integration, and the price is identical monthly and annually — so declining an annual commitment costs nothing. At $9 per user with no seat minimum, you pay $72/month for 8 people and $90/month for 10, both inside the $100 cap, and you can drop back to $72 when the contractors leave. Its 30-day trial is the longest of the three, and unlimited automations, 100 GB storage and built-in time tracking are all above your needs.

**Why not HiveMind Boards.** Cheapest on paper — $49/month flat covers all 10 users — but it has no native GitHub or GitLab integration, only a Zapier connection. That fails a stated must-have, so price does not rescue it. Worth noting for the future: the 11th user forces the $99 Growth plan, which is still under cap but doubles the cost.

**Why not Planlet.** Gantt lives only on the Business plan, and Business billed monthly is $15/user. That is $120/month at 8 users and $150 at 10 — over the hard cap in both cases. The Team plan at $8/user would fit the budget but has no Gantt view. The annual Business rate of $12/user ($96 at 8 users) would squeeze under the cap at 8 people, but finance has ruled out annual commitments, and it would break the cap anyway at 10 users.

One caveat on TaskForge: guests count as full paid seats. If the two contractors could have been guests elsewhere, here they are seats — already accounted for in the $90 figure.
