## Recommendation

Go with **TaskForge Pro** at $9 per user/month — $72/month for the 8-person squad and $90/month at the 10-user peak, both inside the $100 monthly cap, with Gantt and a native GitHub integration included on the only paid plan.

## Why not the others

- **Planlet** — the Gantt/timeline view exists only on Business, which is $15 per user/month on monthly billing: $120 at 8 users and $150 at 10 users, both over the $100 ceiling. The cheaper Team plan ($8 per user/month monthly, $64 at 8 users) has GitHub but no Gantt, so it cannot meet a must-have at any price the team can approve.
- **Hivemind Boards** — cheapest on paper ($49/month flat at both 8 and 10 users) and has Gantt on every plan, but the datasheet states there is no native GitHub or GitLab integration, which breaks the requirement that every task link to a pull request. It also caps Starter at 10 users, so the two contractors put the team exactly at the ceiling with no headroom before a $99/month Growth upgrade.

One caveat on the pick: TaskForge counts guests as full paid seats, so if the two contractors are added as guests rather than members they still cost $9 each — the $90 figure already assumes that. The 5 GB of attachments sits comfortably within TaskForge's 100 GB workspace storage, and the 30-day trial lets the team validate the GitHub link before Finance is billed.
