**Recommendation: TaskForge Pro.**

**Monthly totals (monthly billing)**

| Tool | Plan needed | Rate | 8 users | 10 users |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TaskForge | Pro | $9/user/mo (same monthly or annual) | **$72** | **$90** |
| Planlet | Business (only plan with Gantt) | $15/user/mo monthly | **$120** | **$150** |
| HiveMind | Starter | $49/mo flat, up to 10 users | **$49** | **$49** |

**Why TaskForge**

It is the only tool that meets all four requirements at once. Gantt is included on Pro alongside Kanban, list and calendar. GitHub is a native integration (plus GitLab, Slack, Figma). Pro is $9 per user/month at the same price monthly or annually, so there is no annual commitment and no penalty for avoiding one. At 8 users that is $72/month and at 10 users $90/month, both under the $100 ceiling, with $10/month of headroom at full size. There are no minimum seats, so growth from 8 to 10 is just two more seats.

Beyond the requirements, Pro includes unlimited automations, 100 GB workspace storage, built-in time tracking, a 30-day trial (the longest of the three), and an API capped at 1,000 requests per hour per workspace. The one real trade-off: guests count as full paid seats, so anyone brought in from outside adds $9/month. At 10 users the budget absorbs one guest ($99) and no more.

**Why not Planlet**

Gantt/timeline is on the Business plan only. On monthly billing Business is $15 per user/month, which is $120 at 8 users and $150 at 10 — over budget before the team even grows. The $12 annual rate would fit at 8 users, but that requires the annual commitment the team does not want. The cheaper Team plan is within budget but has no Gantt view, so it does not meet the requirement at all.

**Why not HiveMind Boards**

Pricing is the best of the three — $49/month flat covers all 8 users and still covers 10, well under budget, and Gantt is on every plan. But it has no native GitHub or GitLab integration; GitHub would have to go through Zapier, and the datasheet does not list it as a supported integration. That fails a stated requirement. Two secondary risks reinforce this: the 11th user forces an upgrade to Growth at $99/month, and time tracking exists only through a third-party Zapier connection.
