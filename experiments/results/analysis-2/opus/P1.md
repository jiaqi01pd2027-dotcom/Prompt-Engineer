## Recommendation: TaskForge

**Cost:** $9/user/month with no annual commitment (same price either way) — $72/month at 8 people, $90/month at 10. It stays under $100 even at 11 users ($99), so the growth path is covered.

**Requirements check:**
- Gantt — included on Pro, no upsell tier
- Native GitHub — yes (plus GitLab and Figma)
- Monthly billing, no lock-in — yes; monthly and annual are the same rate, so there's no penalty for staying month-to-month
- Budget — $90/month at the 10-person target, $10 under the cap

**Extras:** unlimited automations, 100 GB storage, built-in time tracking, 30-day trial, API capped at 1,000 requests/hour per workspace.

**One caveat:** guests count as full paid seats. If the team regularly brings in clients or contractors to view boards, each one adds $9/month and eats the remaining headroom fast. If that's a real pattern, budget for it before committing.

## Why not the others

**Planlet** — fails on two counts. Gantt is Business-only, and Business at monthly billing is $15/user, so 8 seats is $120/month, already over budget before the team grows. The annual rate ($12/user, $96 for 8) would squeeze in but requires exactly the annual commitment the team ruled out. The cheaper Team plan at $8/user monthly ($64) is affordable but has no Gantt view at all, so it doesn't meet the core requirement.

**Hivemind Boards** — the pricing is the best fit on paper ($49/month flat covers all 10 users, less than half the budget) and Gantt is on every plan, but there is no native GitHub or GitLab integration. GitHub would have to go through Zapier, which means a third-party dependency, a separate subscription, and a shallower integration than a native one. That's a hard requirement missed. Worth noting also that the 11th user forces a jump to Growth at $99/month, and time tracking is likewise Zapier-only.
