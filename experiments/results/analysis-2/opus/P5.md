## Recommendation

Go with TaskForge on the Pro plan at $9 per user/month, billed monthly.

## Why not the others

- **Planlet** — the Gantt/timeline view is exclusive to the Business plan, and Business on monthly billing costs $120/month at 8 users and $150 at 10, over the $100 ceiling; the only way under budget is the annual commitment the team explicitly ruled out.
- **Hivemind Boards** — it is the cheapest option and has Gantt on every plan, but it has no native GitHub integration (only a Zapier workaround), which is a hard requirement; the Starter plan's 10-user cap also means growth to an 11th person triggers a forced $99/month Growth upgrade.
