## Step 1 — Cheapest plan with both Gantt and native GitHub

| Tool | Gantt available on | Native GitHub | Cheapest qualifying plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planlet | Business only | Yes (Team and above) | Business |
| TaskForge | Pro | Yes | Pro |
| HiveMind Boards | All plans | **No** — Zapier only | none |

HiveMind fails the hard requirement at this step: no plan has native GitHub.

## Step 2 — Monthly cost on monthly billing (no annual commitment)

- **Planlet Business**, $15/user/mo monthly: 8 × $15 = **$120**; 10 × $15 = **$150**
- **TaskForge Pro**, $9/user/mo (same monthly or annual): 8 × $9 = **$72**; 10 × $9 = **$90**
- HiveMind Starter would be $49 flat for up to 10, but it is already disqualified.

## Step 3 — Against the $100/month budget

- Planlet: $120 at 8 users, over budget before the team even grows. Its $12/user annual rate would hit $96 at 8 users, but that requires the annual commitment the team ruled out — and $120 at 10 users annually anyway.
- TaskForge: $72 at 8, $90 at 10. Both fit, with $10/mo headroom at full size.

Only one candidate survives, so there is no second finalist to compare limits against. TaskForge's limits on their own: unlimited automations, 100 GB workspace storage, built-in time tracking, 30-day trial, no seat minimum, API capped at 1,000 requests/hour per workspace, and guests billed as full paid seats.

## Recommendation: TaskForge Pro

Take **TaskForge Pro at $9/user/month**, billed monthly — $72/month for 8 people, $90/month at 10. It is the only option that delivers Gantt, native GitHub, and monthly billing inside $100, and it does so without an annual lock-in penalty (the price is identical either way, so nothing is lost by staying monthly). The extras are real, not filler: unlimited automations, 100 GB storage, and built-in time tracking, plus GitLab and Figma integrations if the stack shifts.

Two things to watch:
- **Guests cost money.** Guests are billed as full seats, so at 10 members only one external collaborator fits under $100. If the team regularly brings in clients or contractors, budget for it or use a shared read-only export instead.
- **Headroom ends at 11.** An 11th member is $99; a 12th puts you at $108, over budget. Plan the budget conversation before the 12th hire.
- The 1,000 req/hour/workspace API cap is generous for a team this size but worth knowing if you build heavy CI reporting against it.

**Why not Planlet:** Gantt is locked to the Business tier, which is $15/user/month on monthly billing — $120 at 8 users and $150 at 10, over budget from day one. The $12/user annual rate ($96 at 8) technically fits but violates the no-annual-commitment requirement, and it breaks the budget anyway once the team reaches 10.

**Why not HiveMind Boards:** The pricing is the most attractive of the three — $49/month flat covers all 10 users — but it has no native GitHub integration at any tier, only a Zapier bridge. That fails a stated requirement outright, and the same Zapier dependency also stands in for time tracking, so two needs ride on a third-party connector. Price cannot rescue a missing must-have.
