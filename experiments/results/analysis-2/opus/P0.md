There are no files to inspect here — this is a comparison of the three spec sheets given. I worked the numbers and the feature gates directly.

## Short answer: TaskForge, for most teams

It is the only one where nothing important is locked behind a higher tier. Gantt, unlimited automations, 100 GB storage, and built-in time tracking all come with the single $9/user/month Pro plan, and it is the only one of the three with time tracking at all (HiveMind's "time tracking" is a Zapier hop to someone else's product). It also has the widest dev integration set (Slack, GitHub, GitLab, Figma), no seat minimum, one price for monthly or annual so there is no lock-in penalty, and the longest trial at 30 days.

## Where each one actually wins

**PLANLET** — best if you work with clients or outside stakeholders. Unlimited free view/comment guests is a real cost difference: 10 guests on TaskForge is 10 paid seats ($90/month), and HiveMind caps you at 5. Its free tier (5 users, 3 projects) is also the most generous. The catch: Gantt/timeline is Business-only, so needing a timeline doubles your price to $12/user/month and there is a 3-seat minimum on paid plans.

**HIVEMIND BOARDS** — best only if you have a large team that does not need GitHub/GitLab. Flat pricing wins on scale: at 25 users, Growth is $82.50/month on annual billing versus $225 for TaskForge and $150 for Planlet Team. Roughly, it becomes the cheapest option above ~5 users versus TaskForge and ~7 users versus Planlet Team. But no native GitHub or GitLab is disqualifying for an engineering team, automations are capped, and there is a hard cliff at user 11 on Starter — the 11th seat forces Growth and takes you from $49 to $99/month.

**TASKFORGE** — everything above, with the two weaknesses being guests billed as full seats and per-user pricing that gets expensive past ~15 people.

## Picking by situation

- Software team, any size under ~15: **TaskForge**.
- Heavy external collaboration, no timeline needed: **Planlet Team** ($6/user/month annual).
- 15+ users, non-engineering work: **HiveMind Growth**.
- Under 5 people, no budget: **Planlet Free**, then move to TaskForge when you need Gantt or time tracking.

One caveat on the data: TaskForge is the only vendor that publishes an API rate limit (1,000 req/hour/workspace). That is not evidence the others are more generous — it usually means they do not disclose, so confirm before building anything against their APIs. Worth verifying seat minimums and guest definitions with sales too, since those are the terms most often different in the contract than on the pricing page.
