**Comparison (8 users, growing to 10)**

| Tool | Plan needed | Cost at 8 users | Cost at 10 users | Gantt | Native GitHub | Monthly billing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Planlet | Business (monthly) | $120 | $150 | yes | yes | yes |
| TaskForge | Pro | $72 | $90 | yes | yes | yes |
| Hivemind Boards | Starter | $49 | $49 | yes | no | yes |

**Recommendation: TaskForge Pro.** It is the only tool that clears all four requirements. Gantt is included in Pro rather than gated behind a higher tier, GitHub is a native integration (plus GitLab and Figma), and the $9/user price is identical monthly or annual, so there is no penalty for avoiding a commitment. At 8 users it costs $72/month, and growth to 10 costs $90/month — still under the $100 cap, with no tier cliff since pricing is purely per-seat. There is also no seat minimum, unlimited automations, 100 GB storage, built-in time tracking, and a 30-day trial. The one caveat is guests: TaskForge bills guests as full paid seats, so if the team regularly brings in external collaborators, each one adds $9/month and eats into the headroom above 10 users.

**Why not Planlet.** The Gantt/timeline view exists only on the Business plan, and Business billed monthly is $15/user. That is $120/month at 8 users — $20 over budget before any growth — and $150/month at 10. The $12/user annual rate would fit ($96 at 8 users), but taking it means an annual commitment, which the team explicitly rules out. So Planlet fails on price precisely because the two requirements interact: the cheap way to get Gantt requires the billing terms you have excluded.

**Why not Hivemind Boards.** It is by far the cheapest at $49/month flat, includes Gantt on every plan, and the flat rate absorbs growth to 10 users at no extra cost. But it has no native GitHub integration — only Slack, Google Drive, and Zapier. GitHub would have to be bridged through Zapier, which is not a native integration and would consume part of the 1,000 automation runs per month on Starter. That is a hard requirement failure, not a degradation. Worth noting for the future: the 11th user forces an upgrade to Growth at $99/month, so the flat-rate advantage disappears if the team grows past 10.
