## Recommendation: TaskForge Pro

It is the only one of the three that meets all four hard requirements at once.

**How it scores against the must-haves**

| | Gantt | Native GitHub | Monthly, no annual lock-in | Cost at 8 / 10 users |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TaskForge Pro** | Yes, on Pro | Yes (plus GitLab, Figma) | $9/user either way — monthly costs no premium | **$72 / $90** |
| Planlet Business | Business plan only | Yes | Monthly available at a 25% markup | $120 / $150 |
| Hivemind Starter | Yes, all plans | **No** — Zapier only | Monthly flat rate | $49 / $49 |

**TaskForge details that matter here**

- Gantt is included on Pro alongside Kanban, list and calendar, so the sprint timeline needs no upgrade tier.
- Native GitHub integration on the same plan, which covers the task-to-PR linking.
- No minimum seat count and identical monthly/annual pricing, so finance gets pure monthly billing with zero penalty for refusing a commitment. Seats scale one at a time: 8 = $72, 10 = $90, and there is even room for an 11th seat at $99 before the $100 cap bites.
- 100 GB workspace storage against roughly 5 GB of attachments — no concern.
- Unlimited automations (unused, but no cap to manage), built-in time tracking, and a 1,000 req/hour workspace API limit.
- 30-day trial, the longest of the three.
- One caveat: guests count as full paid seats. If the two contractors are onboarded as guests rather than members, they still cost $9 each — but that is already priced into the $90 figure, so it changes nothing.

**Why Planlet is out — price**

Gantt lives only on the Business plan, and Business on monthly billing is $15/user/month. That is $120/month for 8 people and $150 at 10 — over budget before the contractors even arrive. The cheaper Team plan ($8/user monthly, $64 at 8 seats) fits the budget but has no Gantt at all, and unlimited guests do not help since guests are view-and-comment only, which does not work for contractors doing sprint work. Note the trap: Business billed annually would be $96 at 8 seats, just under the cap — precisely the annual commitment finance has ruled out.

**Why Hivemind Boards is out — no native GitHub**

At $49/month flat for up to 10 users it is by far the cheapest and its Gantt is on every plan, so on price and views alone it would win. But the datasheet lists no native GitHub or GitLab integration; GitHub would have to be reached through Zapier, which is not a native integration and is not something the datasheet describes as supporting PR linking. Since every task links to a pull request, that is a disqualifying gap, not an inconvenience. Two secondary points reinforce it: the 25 GB Starter storage is fine for 5 GB, but adding an 11th user forces an upgrade to Growth at $99/month, leaving essentially no headroom under the $100 cap, and time tracking is also Zapier-dependent.

**Bottom line:** TaskForge Pro at $72/month today, $90/month if both contractors join, on month-to-month billing with no price penalty and a 30-day trial to validate the GitHub PR linking before committing any spend.
