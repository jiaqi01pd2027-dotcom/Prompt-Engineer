## Recommendation: TaskForge

It is the only one of the three that satisfies all four hard requirements at once.

**How it meets the requirements**

| Requirement | TaskForge |
|---|---|
| Gantt/timeline | "Kanban, list, calendar and Gantt on Pro" — included at the only paid tier |
| Native GitHub | Yes ("Slack, GitHub, GitLab, Figma") |
| Monthly, no annual commitment | "$9 per user/month, same price on monthly or annual billing" — you pay nothing extra for the flexibility |
| Under $100/month | 8 seats = **$72/mo**; 10 seats = **$90/mo** |

Growing 8 → 10 costs $18/mo more and requires no plan change ("No minimum seats"). Extras you get regardless: unlimited automations, 100 GB workspace storage, built-in time tracking, and a 30-day trial — the longest of the three, so you can pilot it for a full sprint cycle before paying.

**Two caveats to plan around**
- **Guests count as full paid seats.** If you regularly bring in contractors or clients, each one is another $9/mo and eats your headroom. Planlet's "unlimited free guests (view and comment only)" is genuinely better here — it just isn't better enough to outweigh the Gantt paywall below.
- **Budget ceiling at 11 seats.** $9 × 11 = $99/mo, right at the line; 12 seats breaks $100. You're covered through the stated growth to 10, with roughly one seat to spare.
- Minor: the API is capped at "1,000 requests per hour per workspace" — fine for normal use, worth knowing if you plan heavy custom scripting.

---

**Why not Planlet — the Gantt view is behind the expensive tier**

Gantt/timeline is "Business only," and Business is "$15 per user/month billed monthly." That's $120/mo for 8 people and $150/mo for 10 — over budget before you reach your current headcount, let alone your target.

The only way under $100 is the annual rate ("$12 per user/month billed annually"), which is $96/mo for 8 — but that requires exactly the annual commitment you ruled out, and it *still* breaks the budget at $120/mo once you hit 10 users. The affordable Team plan ($8/user/month monthly, $64 for 8) doesn't include Gantt at all. Planlet only wins if you drop either the Gantt requirement or the monthly-billing requirement, and dropping the second one doesn't even survive your growth plan.

**Why not HiveMind Boards — no GitHub, and it's not fixable**

"No native GitHub or GitLab integration." This is a hard disqualification, not a tradeoff. Everything else about it fits nicely: Gantt is on "all plans," Starter is "$49 per month flat for up to 10 users" — comfortably under budget, flat pricing that doesn't scale with headcount, and it covers your growth to 10 exactly.

But the only path to GitHub is Zapier, which means paying for a third-party service to approximate what the other two do natively — and you'd be doing the same thing again for time tracking, which is "only via a Zapier connection to a third-party tracker." Two core needs routed through a middleman is a real ongoing tax in cost, latency, and breakage.

Worth flagging even if GitHub weren't an issue: the growth cliff is steep. "Adding an 11th user on Starter requires upgrading to Growth" — one seat past 10 doubles your bill from $49 to $99/mo. Given you're already projecting 10, you'd be sitting right on that edge.

---

**Bottom line:** start TaskForge's 30-day trial with the full team. At $72/mo today and $90/mo at 10 people, you get the Gantt view and native GitHub integration on true month-to-month terms, plus built-in time tracking you'd otherwise be bolting on. Just decide up front how you'll handle external collaborators, since guests bill as full seats.
