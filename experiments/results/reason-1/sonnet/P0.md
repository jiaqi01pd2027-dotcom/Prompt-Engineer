## Solution

**Step 1 — Cost of each item type**
- Pens: 7 × $2.25 = $15.75
- Notebooks: 3 × $5.40 = $16.20

**Step 2 — Apply the 15% coupon (notebooks only)**
- Discount: $16.20 × 0.15 = $2.43
- Discounted notebooks: $16.20 − $2.43 = $13.77

**Step 3 — Subtotal after discount**
- $15.75 (pens, undiscounted) + $13.77 (notebooks, discounted) = **$29.52**

**Step 4 — Apply 8% sales tax to the whole discounted subtotal**
- Tax: $29.52 × 0.08 = $2.3616 → $2.36
- Total due: $29.52 + $2.3616 = **$31.8816 ≈ $31.88**

**Step 5 — Change from a $50 bill**
- $50.00 − $31.8816 = $18.1184

### Answer
**Sarah receives about $18.12 in change** (total bill ≈ $31.88).
