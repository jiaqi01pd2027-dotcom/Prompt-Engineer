## Solution

**Step 1 — Item subtotals**
- Pens: 7 × $2.25 = $15.75
- Notebooks: 3 × $5.40 = $16.20

**Step 2 — Apply coupon (15% off notebooks only)**
- Discount: $16.20 × 0.15 = $2.43
- Discounted notebooks price: $16.20 − $2.43 = $13.77

**Step 3 — Subtotal after discount (pens untouched, notebooks discounted)**
- $15.75 + $13.77 = $29.52

**Step 4 — Apply 8% sales tax to that post-discount subtotal**
- Tax: $29.52 × 0.08 = $2.3616 → rounded to the nearest cent = $2.36

**Step 5 — Total due**
- $29.52 + $2.36 = $31.88

**Step 6 — Change from $50**
- $50.00 − $31.88 = **$18.12**

Sarah receives **$18.12** in change.
