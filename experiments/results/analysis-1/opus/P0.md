**Decisions**

1. **Calendar sync beta ships Google-only, launching October 6** (moved from October 1). Outlook is out of scope for this beta — API permissions took two weeks and token refresh still fails on ~1 in 20 accounts. Sofia's ticket data showed ~80% of sync demand is Google; Outlook is mostly the two enterprise trials. Tom pushed to keep Outlook for the enterprise trials but was overruled. Revisit after beta data.
2. **"Outlook coming soon" is allowed on the page, with no date attached.**
3. **Onboarding flow drops the Outlook tile** (or greys it out with the coming-soon label).
4. **Team plan goes from $8 to $10 per seat for new customers starting November 1.** Existing customers grandfathered at $8 until at least next year, and told proactively. No engineering blockers — plan IDs are already separate.
5. **The weekly sync moves to Wednesdays, same time**, starting next week (this week: skip Monday, meet Wednesday). The Monday slot collided with support handover and Dan's on-call review.
6. **The workspace feature is officially named "Rooms"**, everywhere — over "Spaces" (engineering) and "the folder thing" (support/customers). Rooms tested better in five user interviews.
