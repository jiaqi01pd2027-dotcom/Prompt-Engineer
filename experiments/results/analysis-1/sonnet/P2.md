# Lumen Product Sync — Minutes (Mon, 14 Sept)

## Decisions

1. **Beta scope:** The calendar-sync beta ships with **Google Calendar only**; Outlook is out of scope for this beta (revisit after beta data is in).
2. **Beta launch date:** Moved from October 1 to **October 6** (Google-only).
3. **Messaging:** The product page may say "Outlook coming soon," but without any specific date.
4. **Pricing:** Team plan increases to **$10/seat for new customers, effective November 1**.
5. **Grandfathering:** Existing customers stay at **$8/seat** through at least next year.
6. **Meeting cadence:** Weekly product sync moves from **Monday to Wednesday**, starting next week (this week's Monday session is skipped, not doubled).
7. **Naming:** The workspace feature is officially named **"Rooms"** (not Spaces or "the folder thing").

*Noted but explicitly not decided:* a "maybe" line about revisiting Outlook in Q1 will go on the roadmap doc as a non-commitment; competitor pricing page review was flagged but left unowned.
