Brief: "We want a dashboard so customers can see their usage."

Scope (in): a Usage page in the existing web app showing an account's consumption of its billable metrics (API calls, storage, seats, or whatever the plan meters) for the current billing period, with a daily trend chart for the last 30 days, a comparison against the plan's included quota, and a CSV download of the daily figures. Data refreshed hourly from existing billing/metering events.

Non-goals: real-time usage, per-user or per-project breakdowns, usage alerts and email notifications, forecasting or overage estimates, a usage API, custom date ranges beyond the last 30 days, dashboards for internal staff.

Assumptions and open questions: (assumption) metering events already exist for billing and are the source of truth, so no new instrumentation is needed; (assumption) an hourly aggregation job is acceptable latency; (question) which metrics do customers actually ask support about, and are they the same ones we bill on? (question) do the numbers have to reconcile exactly with the invoice, or is "close and clearly labeled as provisional" acceptable? (question) which roles in an account may see usage, and does it include cost or only quantities?

Milestones: 1. hourly aggregation job and per-account daily rollup table, backfilled 30 days, 1 week. 2. read endpoint with quota comparison and CSV download, 3 days. 3. UI page with trend chart and current-period summary, 1 week. 4. reconcile rollups against last month's invoices for a sample of accounts, fix discrepancies, and release behind a flag to 10% of accounts, 4 days.

Risks: dashboard disagrees with the invoice and generates support tickets rather than reducing them: reconcile in milestone 4 before release and label figures as provisional until the period closes. Aggregation over the largest accounts is slow or lags: roll up incrementally per hour rather than recomputing the period, and index by account and day. Rollup table grows without bound: retain daily rows for 13 months and drop hourly detail after 30 days. Customers see usage they cannot act on and ask for alerts and breakdowns immediately: log the requests and keep them as the explicit next phase rather than pulling them into v1.
