It's built to be scanned in a review meeting, not read line by line:

- **Scope** — the must-haves and non-goals from the PM answers, side by side, so nothing gets silently re-added mid-build.
- **Approach** — the one piece of new schema (a `monthly_usage_rollup` table), why the API only ever reads from it, and how the nightly job, CSV export, and 80%-alert all share that one source of truth. No new infra.
- **Milestones** — a 6-week timeline (Sep 14 – Oct 23) that deliberately pilots on Oct 9, ten days ahead of the PM's Oct 19 ask, so there are two full weeks of real pilot usage before the 1,200-account rollout instead of pilot and full launch landing in the same week.
- **Risks**, split into two tiers because they need different action:
  - *Blocking* (resolve before week 1 ends): the 6-month trend needs a one-time backfill script, not just the forward-looking nightly job; the brief doesn't say where per-metric plan limits are stored; "current month" needs to be confirmed against the billing pipeline's actual month boundary (calendar vs. anniversary).
  - *Watch*: alert re-notification needs dedup state so it doesn't fire nightly; raw-table query performance is unverified until real volume is checked; there's no QA buffer beyond the deliberately-empty week 5.

The three "blocking" items are flagged as the concrete asks for the meeting — they're gaps in the brief itself, not engineering unknowns, so they're worth resolving before the team commits to the 6-week estimate.
